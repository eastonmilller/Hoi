OFFICER R. HALE · BADGE 4471 · PERSONAL LOG

DAY 1

Locked the door at 02:20. Watched a guard go down on CAM 2. Nobody answers on channel 4.

DAY 2

Water: 11 bottles. Rations: 9. Pushed the cabinets against the door. Something walked past at 03:00. It did not knock.

DAY 3

Channel 2 went quiet. There is a man's voice in the vents at night. Calm. Like a doctor doing his rounds.

DAY 4

PA says Gate A is sealed. Evacuation complete. They left us here.

DAY 5

Burning smell all day. CAM 3 shows the 457 chamber glowing through the glass.

DAY 6

Something scratched at the barricade for an hour. Then a voice through the door said patience. Then nothing.

DAY 7

Last ration. Two bottles. If anyone finds this: my name is Hale. I did my job.
